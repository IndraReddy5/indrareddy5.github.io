Spotlight is a music sharing platform built to close the gap between independent creators and their audience. Artists can upload tracks, manage their profile, and get visibility without relying on algorithm-heavy streaming platforms. Listeners get a clean discovery interface to browse and play music directly in the browser.

**What it does**

- Artist accounts with track upload and profile management
- In-browser audio playback
- Discovery feed for listeners to browse tracks by genre and artist
- REST API backend serving the Vue frontend

**Stack:** Vue.js, Python, REST API
